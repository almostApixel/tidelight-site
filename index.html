The story

A storm took the harbour.

The pier still stands, and so does the little yellow sub.

Dive the reef below, bring home what you find, and raise the harbour again one building at a time. Somewhere in the keeper's lost logbook is what really happened the night of the storm. 💛

How it plays

Dive, haul, rebuild, repeat

Every dive is a little adventure, and every trip home makes the harbour a bit cozier.

1Dive

  • The crane lowers your sub into a reef that's new every dive.
  • Seven zones down past 900 m, each with a rule of its own: kelp currents, stinging fire coral, caves where your lamps reach less far, vents that blast hot water.
  • Watch your battery and your air. Push too far and you're towed home, losing part of the haul, never your progress.

2Haul

  • Swim out as the diver to net the fish you meet, from clownfish to coelacanths, and the odd golden one.
  • Gather shells, kelp, coral and crystals from the rock, and open the chests tucked away in the reef.
  • Sharks are an obstacle, not a killer: a bite costs air or a dent in the hull.

3Explore

  • Squeeze the diver through crawls no sub fits.
  • Wash rubble away with a water jet and find the hidden rooms behind it.
  • Lost logbook pages and the town's keepsakes wait in the deep.

4Rebuild

  • Six storm-wrecked buildings to raise again, in any order you like, each growing bigger as you upgrade it.
  • Spend your haul on a better sub and a better suit, and go a little deeper.
  • Bring the whole harbour back, then keep diving for as long as you like.
The aquarium

Your own walk-in aquarium

Every fish you bring home can swim in your hall. Visitors wander past the tanks, and your fish earn coins every minute you play. Nothing you neglect ever comes to harm.
